## Highest-priority permanent corpus rule

User instruction dated 2026-10-24: **对于语料，永远绝不允许机械复核。** All corpus originals, expected results, annotations, source mappings and assets must be reviewed through actual visual reading of the original pages and direct comparison with the current authored material. This permanent execution rule applies to every future task, prompt and delegated agent; it supersedes any local instruction allowing mechanical corpus review or validation.

Never create, restore, retain or run corpus review scripts, temporary one-off checkers, character or number matching, text-layer extraction, OCR, parser-based verification, coordinate or grid heuristics, automated hash/count/format/link/mapping acceptance, or automated content pass/fail rules. Do not replace them with another programming language, tool, service or model. Existing mechanical results cannot establish that corpus content is correct, complete, visually reviewed or accepted. Generic repository checks must exclude all corpus materials, including explicit-path invocations.

Low-precision mechanical findings can falsely label correct content as wrong, cause destructive edits and create repeated correction cycles. Never treat a finding, proposed replacement or earlier review record as source truth. Visually verify the original before deciding whether any edit is needed, preserve already-correct content, withdraw false reports, and restore source-faithful wording when a previous correction introduced an error. An unresolved visual ambiguity remains unknown; it is not permission to normalize, infer or force a change.

Tools may display, render, rotate and enlarge original pages and read or save authored files. A reviewer must actually inspect every page and issue claimed as reviewed, record the physical pages and source-backed judgment, and leave unseen or unreadable content pending or unknown. Necessary metadata updates after a visual edit only save that edit; they must not compare or accept the corpus, infer source mappings, or generate judgments. Keep preview functionality separate from review and preserve historical records without treating them as new evidence. Corpus-specific prompts and operating details are maintained in [the corpus instructions](pdf_sample/parsing/AGENTS.md).

User clarification and implementation authorization dated 2026-10-24: automated scoring of a tested pipeline's outputs against frozen, visually confirmed references is allowed. This is a narrow evaluation permission, not permission to mechanically review, accept, generate or rewrite originals, expected results, annotations, mappings or assets. The scorer must be read-only with respect to reference materials and must not offer corpus checking or repair commands. Unseen, ambiguous and unannotated content remains pending or unknown. Packaging may copy the explicitly authored selection and extract its declared PDF pages without interpreting their contents. Never infer review completion from packaging, hashes, counts, links, schemas or evaluation scores.

Read selected backends' native results faithfully; distinguish parser defects from integration, conversion and assembly defects.
